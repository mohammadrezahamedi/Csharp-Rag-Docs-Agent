FileNest Refund Rules

FileNest is an app that stores your files online. This page explains when you can get your money back.

Monthly Plans
If you pay every month, you can ask for a refund within 7 days of the payment.
After 7 days, we cannot refund that month, but you can cancel so you are not charged again.

Yearly Plans
If you pay once a year, you can ask for a refund within 30 days of the payment.
After 30 days, we refund the unused months only if you had a technical problem that we could not fix.

How Fast Is the Refund?
Once we approve a refund, the money goes back to the same card you used.
It usually arrives in 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

What We Do Not Refund
We do not refund extra storage you bought as a one-time add-on if you already used more than half of it.
We do not refund payments made through the Apple App Store or Google Play. For those, please ask Apple or Google directly.

How to Ask for a Refund
Open the app, go to Settings, then Billing, and press the Request Refund button.
You can also write to billing@filenest.example and include the email address of your account.
You do not need to explain a reason, but it helps us improve.
