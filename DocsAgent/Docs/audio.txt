Audio Design Notes

All audio in the project uses spatial sound to make the VR world feel real.

Spatial Audio
The Meta XR Audio SDK handles spatialization with HRTF filtering.
Each audio source has a maximum distance of 20 meters with logarithmic rolloff.
Footstep sounds change depending on the surface type: wood, metal, concrete or grass.

Music
Background music is streamed from disk and never fully loaded into memory.
Music volume is 30 percent lower than the sound effects by default.
The music fades out over 2 seconds when a fight begins and fades back in after the fight.

Sound Effects
Short effects are compressed with ADPCM and loaded into memory at startup.
The maximum number of simultaneous voices is 24 on Quest 2.
Gunshot sounds use three random variations to avoid repetition.
A low pass filter is applied when the player is underwater.

Mixer Setup
The audio mixer has four groups: Master, Music, SFX and Voice.
The player can change each group volume in the settings menu.
The Voice group is ducked by 6 decibels when a tutorial message plays.

Voice Chat
Voice chat quality is set to 16 kHz mono to save bandwidth.
Push to talk is optional and mapped to the left controller menu button.
