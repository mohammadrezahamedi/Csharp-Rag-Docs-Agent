FileNest Support Hours and Reply Times

This page tells you when our team is available and how fast we usually answer.

Support Hours
Our support team works Monday to Friday, from 9 AM to 6 PM Eastern Time.
We are closed on weekends and on public holidays in the United States.
Messages sent while we are closed are answered on the next working day.

How to Contact Us
The fastest way is the chat button inside the app.
You can also send an email to help@filenest.example.
We do not offer phone support for free accounts. Only Business plan customers can call us.

Reply Times
Free accounts get a first reply within 48 hours.
Pro accounts get a first reply within 12 hours.
Business accounts get a first reply within 2 hours during support hours.

Urgent Problems
If you cannot open any of your files at all, mark your message as Urgent.
Urgent messages from Pro and Business accounts are answered first.
If the whole FileNest service is down, check status.filenest.example for live updates before writing to us.
