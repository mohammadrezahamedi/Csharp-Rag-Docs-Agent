FileNest Frequently Asked Questions

How much storage do I get?
Free accounts get 5 GB. Pro accounts get 2 TB. Business accounts get 10 TB per user.

How much does it cost?
Pro costs 9 dollars per month, or 90 dollars per year. Business costs 15 dollars per user per month.

What is the biggest file I can upload?
Free accounts can upload files up to 2 GB. Pro and Business accounts can upload files up to 50 GB.

I deleted a file by mistake. Can I get it back?
Yes. Deleted files stay in the Trash folder for 30 days. Open Trash, select the file, and press Restore.
After 30 days the file is gone forever and we cannot recover it.

How do I share a file?
Right click the file and choose Share. You can send a link to anyone.
Pro and Business accounts can add a password to the link and set an expiry date.

I forgot my password. What now?
Press Forgot Password on the login screen. We send a reset link to your email, and the link works for 1 hour.

Can I use FileNest on my phone?
Yes. There are apps for iPhone and Android. Your files sync between all your devices automatically.

Is my data safe?
All files are encrypted when they travel and when they are stored.
